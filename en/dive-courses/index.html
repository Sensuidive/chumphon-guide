PADI dive courses and fun dives in Chumphon: prices

Run by a former saturation diver and IMCA Air Diving Supervisor · Sensui since 2019. Courses and fun dives in English, Chinese, Thai and Malay.

Try it

  • PADI Bubblemaker 3,500 THB
    For kids aged 8 and up. A first try of scuba in the pool, no deeper than 2 m, with an instructor.
  • Discover Scuba Diving 4,000 THB
    No experience needed. 1 pool session and 1 boat dive with an instructor. Extra dives 1,000 each.
  • Snorkelling trip 1,500 THB
    To Mu Ko Chumphon National Park. Park fee extra: Thai 40, foreigner 200 (children 20 / 100).

Get certified

  • PADI Scuba Diver 12,000 THB
    A shorter first certification. Dive to 12 m with a dive professional. Upgrade to Open Water later.
  • PADI Open Water Diver 14,500 THB
    Your first certification, for diving to 18 m anywhere in the world. Includes 6 dives (4 course + 2 fun dives).

Go further

  • PADI Advanced Open Water 13,500 THB
    Your next level, for diving to 30 m. Includes 6 dives.
  • Emergency First Response 7,000 THB
    First aid and CPR for divers and non-divers. Needed for Rescue Diver.
  • PADI Rescue Diver 12,500 THB
    Learn to prevent and handle problems in the water. For Advanced Open Water divers. Includes 3 dives. Emergency First Response needed (within 24 months).

Go pro

  • PADI Divemaster 45,000 THB
    Your first professional level: guide certified divers and help instructors. Start with Rescue Diver and 40 logged dives.

Fun diving

  • Fun Dives, 3 dives 4,500 THB
    Meals, drinks, towels, insurance and transfer included. Extra: national park fee, equipment 500, dive computer 300, torch 200.
  • Scuba Refresher 3,500 THB
    For certified divers who have not dived for 6 months or more.
  • Weekend Packages from 12,900 THB
    2 nights, 5 dives 12,900 · 3 nights, 6 dives 14,900, twin share. Includes hotel, meals, drinks, snacks, park fees, insurance, transfers both ways. Equipment 500 a day.

<b>Singapore-style breakfast and lunch on board</b>, cooked by our own Chumphon Kopi Tiam, plus drip coffee for coffee lovers. <b>No single-use plastic with our meals:</b> reusable lunch boxes and cutlery.
