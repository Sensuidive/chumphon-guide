How we dive with whale sharks

  • Keep at least 3 m from its head and 4 m from its tail. No flash.
  • Never touch, ride, chase or feed it. It is a reserved species under Thai law.
  • Cameras only with Advanced Open Water or 40+ logged dives (Thai law, 2025).
  • Do not block its path. Let it swim away.
